Materi Describing People (Fase D)

Subject Pronouns (Kata Ganti Subjek)

Kata ganti subjek digunakan untuk menggantikan nama orang atau benda:

I = Saya
You = Kamu
He = Dia (Laki-laki)
She = Dia (Perempuan)
It = Itu (Benda/Hewan)
We = Kami / Kita
They = Mereka

Contoh Kalimat:

  • He is tall. (Dia laki-laki tinggi.)
  • She is kind. (Dia perempuan baik hati.)
  • They are friendly. (Mereka ramah.)

To Be (am, is, are)

To be digunakan untuk menghubungkan subjek dengan sifat atau identitas:

I + AM Contoh: I am smart.
He / She / It + IS Contoh: She is tall.
You / We / They + ARE Contoh: They are friendly.

Have and Has (Mempunyai)

Digunakan untuk mendeskripsikan kepemilikan ciri fisik (rambut, mata, dll):

I / You / We / They + HAVE Contoh: They have black eyes.
He / She / It + HAS Contoh: She has long hair.

Adjectives (Kata Sifat)

Physical Appearance (Penampilan Physical)

  • • Tall = Tinggi
  • • Short = Pendek
  • • Slim = Langsing
  • • Curly = Keriting
  • • Straight = Lurus
  • • Long = Panjang

Personality (Kepribadian)

  • • Kind = Baik hati
  • • Friendly = Ramah
  • • Smart = Pintar
  • • Funny = Lucu
  • • Shy = Pemalu
  • • Helpful = Suka menolong
